City data derived from the GeoNames Gazetteer (https://www.geonames.org/),
`cities1000.zip` — all cities with population > 1000 or seats of an
administrative division down to PPLA3. This work is licensed under a
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). GeoNames is a project of
Unxos GmbH, St. Gallen, Switzerland. The data is provided "as is" without
warranty or any representation of accuracy, timeliness or completeness.
The database was filtered and re-formatted for Tawadu; the source records
were otherwise unmodified.

Indication of changes (CC BY 4.0 §3.a.2), extended to the DERIVED database
per docs/phase5-city-audit.md §4: the raw dump was trimmed to a derived
SQLite database (assets/cities.db) — 19-column geoname order parsed
(timezone = column 18), search-normalization columns and indexes added,
the 28 curated Tawadu display names (Arabic with diacritics) applied as
overrides, and alternate names re-keyed as normalized search aliases.
Source records (ids, names, coordinates, population, timezones) were
otherwise unmodified. The raw zip is NOT redistributed in this repository.
