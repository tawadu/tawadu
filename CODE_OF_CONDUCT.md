# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Tawadu to be a welcoming, patient place to contribute — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Standards

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, no personal attacks, no publishing others' private information.
- No political or sectarian arguments — this is a tool for worship, not a forum.
- Accept "I don't know" and "I was wrong" gracefully.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the maintainers (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Maintainers may warn, or remove, ban, or escalate as needed. Enforcement is at the maintainers' discretion and is final.
