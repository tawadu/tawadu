01 — Logo

Three marks, one idea

The mark abstracts the mihrab niche and the worshipper into a single arch and a single dot — standing in the niche, bowed. Geometry is deliberately low and wide: nothing rises too high, nothing dominates.

Recommended

A · Niche

Arch + centred dot. Reads as a mihrab with a worshipper, or a bowed head under an arch. Calm, architectural, ownable.

Alternative

B · Bow

A single rukuʿ arc over the dot. More figurative and softer, but slightly less distinctive at small sizes.

Alternative

C · Two dots

Niche + the two dots of the letter tāʾ (ت). Ties to the wordmark, but risks reading as a face/emoji.

Lockups

Primary · horizontal

Mark left, Latin wordmark right. Used in the app title bar, website, README.

Arabic lockup

For Arabic-first contexts and RTL layouts. Same mark, Arabic wordmark.

Mono on accent

Single-colour mark for trays, notifications, favicons and store icons.

One family, two scripts

09 — Rules

Do / don't

Do
  • Let one idea lead each surface.
  • Use the accent for state and action only.
  • Keep Arabic and Latin on the same baseline.
  • Prefer weight and space over boxes.
  • Design the empty, loading and error states.
Don't
  • No gradients, glass, or neon.
  • No emoji as icons.
  • No centred hero on a Monitor surface.
  • No all-caps Latin labels on RTL layouts.
  • No animation that loops without purpose.
