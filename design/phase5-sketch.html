HIGH-LATITUDE RULE
Angle-based
Estimates Fajr and Isha from the sun's angle — a common choice for high latitudes.
Middle of the night
Places Fajr and Isha at the midpoint between sunset and sunrise.
Seventh of the night
Places Fajr and Isha one seventh of the night after sunset / before sunrise.
These rules only matter when the sun doesn't reach the usual angles — near the polar circles. They bound Fajr/Isha; they cannot create a sunrise that doesn't exist.
